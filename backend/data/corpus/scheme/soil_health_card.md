# Soil Health Card Scheme

The Soil Health Card scheme gives farmers a free laboratory test of their soil
and a card with crop-wise fertiliser recommendations, so they apply the
nutrients their soil actually needs instead of guessing.

## Benefits
A free soil test and a Soil Health Card reporting 12 parameters: nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, zinc, iron, copper, manganese, boron, pH, electrical conductivity and organic carbon. The card recommends fertiliser and organic manure doses for the crops you grow, which cuts fertiliser costs and prevents nutrient imbalance.

## Eligibility
All farmers in India. The state agriculture department collects samples on a grid, roughly one sample per 2.5 hectares of irrigated land and one per 10 hectares of rainfed land.

## Documents required
- Farmer name, mobile number and Aadhaar details
- Survey or khasra number of the field sampled

## How to apply
1. Ask the local agriculture officer, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or soil testing laboratory for a soil test.
2. Collect the sample as instructed, or let department staff collect it: soil from 8 to 10 spots at plough depth, mixed, with about half a kilogram sent to the laboratory.
3. The sample is analysed at a soil testing laboratory.
4. Download the card from the Soil Health Card portal (soilhealth.dac.gov.in) or collect it from the agriculture office.
5. Follow the recommended doses, and retest the field every two to three years.
