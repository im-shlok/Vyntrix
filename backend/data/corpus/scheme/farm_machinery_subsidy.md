# Farm Machinery and Drone Subsidy (SMAM)

The Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) subsidises the purchase of
farm machinery and the setting up of custom hiring centres, so small farmers
can mechanise without having to own costly equipment. Agricultural drones are
covered too.

## Benefits
Individual farmers usually get 40% to 50% of the machine cost as subsidy, with the higher rate for small and marginal, SC/ST, women and north-eastern farmers, covering machines such as tractors, power tillers, rotavators and seed drills. Custom hiring centres and farm machinery banks set up by cooperatives, farmer producer organisations and panchayats get higher assistance, up to 80% for village-level farm machinery banks. Kisan drones are subsidised for farmer producer organisations, custom hiring centres and individual farmers at rates notified by the state.

## Eligibility
Registered farmers with agricultural land, plus farmer groups, cooperatives, farmer producer organisations and rural entrepreneurs setting up custom hiring centres. Small and marginal farmers, women and SC/ST farmers get priority.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land records
- Bank account details
- Caste certificate, if applying under the SC/ST category
- Quotation for the machine from an approved dealer

## How to apply
1. Apply on your state's agriculture DBT portal or the national farm mechanisation portal (agrimachinery.nic.in) when applications open.
2. Choose an approved machine model and a registered dealer.
3. Wait for approval or lottery selection before buying, since machines bought before approval usually do not qualify.
4. Buy the machine and upload the invoice and photographs.
5. The subsidy is credited to your bank account after physical verification.
