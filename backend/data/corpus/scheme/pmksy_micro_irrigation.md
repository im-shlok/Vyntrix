# Per Drop More Crop: Drip and Sprinkler Subsidy (PMKSY)

The Per Drop More Crop component of the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana
(PMKSY) subsidises drip and sprinkler irrigation systems to make every drop of
farm water go further.

## Benefits
A subsidy of 55% of the system cost for small and marginal farmers and 45% for other farmers, shared between the central and state governments; several states add a top-up on top of this. Drip irrigation typically saves 30% to 50% of water compared with flood irrigation, and raises yields through fertigation.

## Eligibility
All categories of farmers who have their own land and a water source, including members of cooperatives, self-help groups and farmer producer organisations.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land records
- Bank account details
- Proof of a water source
- System design and quotation from a registered micro-irrigation company

## How to apply
1. Apply on your state horticulture or agriculture department's micro-irrigation portal, or at the agriculture office.
2. A registered micro-irrigation company surveys the field and prepares the system design and quotation.
3. After approval, the company installs the system.
4. Officials inspect the installation, and the subsidy is released to the farmer or the company according to state rules.
