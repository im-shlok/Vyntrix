# PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps for Farmers)

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan (PM-KUSUM) helps
farmers replace diesel and grid-powered pumps with solar irrigation and earn
income from solar power.

## Benefits
Component B supports standalone off-grid solar pumps, typically up to 7.5 HP. The central government pays 30% of the benchmark cost (50% in north-eastern and hill states), the state government pays at least 30%, and the farmer pays the rest, part of which can be a bank loan. Component C supports solar panels for existing grid-connected pumps, so farmers can sell surplus power to the electricity company. Component A supports small solar power plants of up to 2 MW on barren or fallow land, giving the landowner lease or sale income.

## Eligibility
Individual farmers, groups of farmers, farmer producer organisations, water user associations, cooperatives and panchayats. Exact terms and priorities differ by state, and many states give preference to farmers who use micro-irrigation.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land records for the field where the pump will be installed
- Bank account details
- Passport-size photograph
- Details of the water source, such as a borewell or open well

## How to apply
1. Apply only through your state's nodal agency, usually the state renewable energy development agency or the agriculture department, or through the official MNRE PM-KUSUM website.
2. Submit the application with land and water source details, and pay the farmer's share once the application is approved.
3. An empanelled vendor installs the pump, and officials inspect it before the subsidy is released.
4. Beware of fake PM-KUSUM websites and callers who ask for registration fees. The government has repeatedly warned farmers about these frauds.
