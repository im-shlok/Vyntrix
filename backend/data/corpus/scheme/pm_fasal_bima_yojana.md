# PM Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) insures farmers against crop loss from
natural calamities, pests and diseases. The farmer pays a small share of the
premium and the central and state governments pay the rest.

## Benefits
The farmer's premium is only 2% of the sum insured for kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops. Cover includes prevented sowing; standing crop losses from drought, dry spells, floods, inundation, pests, diseases, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storms, hailstorms and cyclones; post-harvest losses from cyclone and unseasonal rain for up to 14 days; and localised calamities such as hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst and natural fire.

## Eligibility
All farmers, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers, who grow notified crops in notified areas. Enrolment has been voluntary for all farmers, including those with crop loans, since kharif 2020.

## Documents required
- Land records (record of rights), or a tenancy or sharecropping agreement
- Sowing certificate or self-declaration of the crop sown, as required by the state
- Bank passbook or account details
- Aadhaar card

## How to apply
1. Enrol before the cut-off date your state notifies for the season, through your bank, a Common Service Centre, an insurance company agent, or the national crop insurance portal (pmfby.gov.in).
2. Declare the crop and area sown, and pay your share of the premium.
3. Keep the policy receipt, and check the policy status on the portal or the Crop Insurance app.
4. For localised calamities or post-harvest losses, report the loss within 72 hours through the Crop Insurance app, the insurer's helpline, your bank or the agriculture office.
5. Claims are paid directly into your bank account. Losses from widespread calamities are assessed through crop-cutting experiments across the whole insurance unit.
