# e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

e-NAM is an online trading platform that links agricultural produce markets
(APMC mandis) across India, so farmers can sell to more buyers through
transparent online bidding.

## Benefits
Transparent price discovery through online bidding, access to buyers beyond the local mandi, quality testing of produce, and payment directly into the farmer's bank account. Farmers can check prices in linked mandis on the e-NAM website and mobile app before deciding when and where to sell.

## Eligibility
All farmers selling produce in an e-NAM linked mandi, and farmer producer organisations. More than 1,000 mandis across most states are linked to the platform.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card or other identity proof
- Bank account details, such as a passbook or cancelled cheque
- Mobile number

## How to apply
1. Register on the e-NAM portal (enam.gov.in), in the e-NAM mobile app, or at the e-NAM help desk in a linked mandi.
2. Get the registration approved by the mandi (APMC).
3. Bring produce to the mandi, where it is recorded at gate entry and tested for quality.
4. Accept the best online bid, and receive payment directly in your bank account.
