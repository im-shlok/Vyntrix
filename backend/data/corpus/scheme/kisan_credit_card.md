# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

The Kisan Credit Card gives farmers timely, low-interest short-term credit for
cultivation and related needs. It works as a revolving credit limit with the
bank, so the farmer can draw and repay money as needed through the season.

## Benefits
Credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, marketing of produce, household needs, maintenance of farm assets, and working capital for animal husbandry and fisheries. Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme, short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh carry 7% interest, which falls to an effective 4% when repaid on time; the Union Budget 2025-26 announced an increase of this limit to Rs 5 lakh. RBI rules allow agricultural loans up to Rs 2 lakh without collateral from January 2025. Confirm current limits and rates with your bank.

## Eligibility
- Owner cultivators, individual or joint
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers
- Self-help groups and joint liability groups of farmers
- Farmers engaged in animal husbandry and fisheries

## Documents required
- Completed KCC application form
- Identity proof such as an Aadhaar card, voter ID or PAN card
- Address proof
- Land records or proof of cultivation
- Passport-size photograph

## How to apply
1. Visit a commercial bank, regional rural bank, small finance bank or cooperative bank branch, or apply online through the bank's portal where available.
2. PM-KISAN beneficiaries can use the simplified one-page KCC application form available at banks and on the PM-KISAN portal.
3. Submit the form with your land and identity documents.
4. The bank sets the credit limit from the crops grown, the area and the local scale of finance, then issues the card.
5. Repay on time every year to keep the interest benefit and renew the limit.
