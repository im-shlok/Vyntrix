# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

PM-KISAN is a central government scheme that gives direct income support to
landholding farmer families across India. The money is transferred directly
into the farmer's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

## Benefits
Rs 6,000 per year per farmer family, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 through Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Eligibility
All landholding farmer families (husband, wife and minor children) who own cultivable land in their names are eligible, whatever the size of the holding. The scheme was limited to small and marginal farmers when it launched, but was extended to all landholding farmers in June 2019. A family is excluded if any member falls into one of these categories:
- Institutional landholders
- Current or former holders of constitutional posts, ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors and district panchayat chairpersons
- Serving or retired central or state government employees, except Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees
- Retired pensioners receiving Rs 10,000 or more per month, except Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees
- People who paid income tax in the last assessment year
- Registered practising professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land ownership records (khatauni, record of rights or land passbook)
- Bank account seeded with Aadhaar
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

## How to apply
1. Register through "New Farmer Registration" on the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in), the PM-KISAN mobile app, or at a Common Service Centre.
2. Enter your Aadhaar, mobile number, bank details and land record details.
3. Complete e-KYC by OTP on the Aadhaar-linked mobile, biometric verification at a CSC, or face authentication in the mobile app.
4. The state government verifies land records and eligibility before payments start.
5. Track the application and payments with "Know Your Status" on the portal.

## Common problems
Instalments are most often stopped by incomplete e-KYC, a bank account not
seeded with Aadhaar, or a mismatch between the name in Aadhaar, the bank
records and the land records. Correct these at the bank, a CSC or the
agriculture office.
