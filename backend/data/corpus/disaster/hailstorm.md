# Hailstorm Preparedness and Crop Recovery

Hailstorms strike suddenly, mostly between February and May, and can destroy
wheat, mustard, chickpea, vegetables and orchard fruit within minutes. Central
and north-western India and the orchard regions of the Himalayan states are
most affected. Little can be done once hail begins, so preparation and fast
reporting matter most.

## Steps
1. Watch IMD thunderstorm and hail warnings, and harvest mature crops early when a hail warning is issued.
2. Move harvested produce, fodder and animals under cover before the storm.
3. Shelter indoors during hail. Large hailstones can seriously injure people and animals in the open.
4. Photograph the damage the same day, and report the loss to your insurer, bank or agriculture office within 72 hours.
5. Do not plough in or destroy damaged crops until the insurance survey is complete.
6. Remove broken branches and badly damaged fruit, and ask the Krishi Vigyan Kendra about a protective spray, since hail wounds let fungal and bacterial infections in.

## Protecting orchards
Anti-hail nets over apple, grape and other orchards are the most reliable
protection, and several states subsidise them under their horticulture
schemes.
